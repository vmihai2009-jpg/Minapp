# What's new in Minapp

## 1.8.8
- The update light bulb now sits over the menu symbol in the top-left corner. Right-click still opens the menu.
- After an update, Minapp shows what's new (this list).

## 1.8.7
- A glowing light bulb appears in the top-left corner when a new version is ready. Click it to update.

## 1.8.6
- Pause and Stop on the download bar now really stop the download.
- New option in the Local library menu: save downloads as MP3 320, which plays in every DJ app. On by default.

## 1.8.5
- Test release for the in-app updater.

## 1.8.4
- Updating from inside the app now works on Windows while Minapp is running.

## 1.8.3
- While saving a YouTube link, a bar under the player shows which song is downloading, with Pause and Stop buttons in the skin's own style.

## 1.8.2
- Mac: the visualizer reacts to the music and Milkdrop runs smoother.
- Widget mode (small and large) and a macOS desktop widget.
- YouTube sign-in to play your own playlists, and an experimental YouTube Music source.

## 1.8.1
- Long YouTube playlists (500+ songs) save completely. When YouTube asks to slow down, Minapp waits and carries on.
- "Check for updates…" in the menu updates Minapp from GitHub.
