# What's new in Minapp

## 1.9.29
- Fixed the double-size album picture covering the left side of the playlist. The playlist now sits beside the big picture (and runs under it only when the equalizer is open and there is room). The tabs also fit when the playlist is narrow.

## 1.9.28
- New PLAYLIST tab: what's playing, in order, like the classic playlist. Click a song to jump to it.
- The playlist opens much taller (about 14 rows). The small arrow at the end of the top bar hides the bottom buttons for even more room.
- No more blue fills: the row under the pointer and the picked one just turn white. Long titles slide along while you point at them.
- Drag the bottom edge of the playlist up or down to make it shorter or taller.
- The playlist timer shows the playing song's time.
- MENU opens Minapp's own menu in the skin's look (click MENU again, click outside or press Esc to close).

## 1.9.27
- The YouTube Music window no longer pops up by itself (it was being shown each time Minapp read your YT Music library).

## 1.9.26
- A thin bar on top of the playlist is always there: LIBRARY, LYRICS, ARTIST and ALBUM. Lyrics and artist/album info now show right inside the playlist (no separate INFO panel).
- With the album picture open, the playlist stretches all the way under it, so your libraries sit under the cassette. Roll the player up into a bar and the playlist goes back to its normal size.
- Playlist buttons: COPY is now ADD (puts the playing song into the playlist picked on the left). Copying a whole playlist moved to right-click and asks first. INFO is now MENU.
- The player's repeat button is a repeat button again.
- Clicking a song in Spotify Liked songs plays it.
- The YT Music folder now shows your real YT Music playlists and songs.

## 1.9.25
- The playlist window is back, now with your libraries inside: Spotify, YouTube, YT Music and local playlists on the left, their songs (or what's playing now) on the right. Double-click a playlist to play it all.
- Its buttons are now FIND, LIKE, COPY (copy a playlist between Spotify and YouTube / YT Music), SORT and INFO, drawn from your skin's own buttons.
- YT Music: your playlists and Liked music, played in YouTube Music.
- Spotify-made playlists (Daily Mix, Discover Weekly...): add them with "Add a Spotify-made playlist" by pasting their link.
- More songs in view, and the skin's scrollbar now scrolls the list.
- The INFO panel wears your skin's playlist frame.

## 1.9.24
- The PL button is now LIB: it opens and closes your Libraries (Spotify, YouTube and local playlists).
- INFO has a new QUEUE tab instead of the old playlist window, with FIND (type to filter, Enter plays, Esc closes), LIKE, SKIP, SORT (shuffle, A-Z, by artist, reverse) and LISTS.
- LIKE hearts a Spotify song, saves a YouTube song to a "Minapp likes" playlist, and copies a local song to "Liked". Right-click a queued song to hide it.
- Libraries: the magnifier on a playlist searches the songs inside it.
- The panels take the player's colour from each skin, and the letters sit cleanly on it. Switching skins now updates the INFO and LIB buttons too.

## 1.9.23
- Clicking a song in a Spotify search now plays it.
- Search: a long text scrolls so you always see what you type, and a SHOW drop-down filters Spotify results to songs, albums or artists.
- ALBUM tab: click any song in the list to play it.
- The title and clock no longer get stuck on an earlier song.
- The INFO button's word sits on the button's own surface, with no box behind it.

## 1.9.22
- LIBRARIES: the playlist panel now sits tucked under the album picture, level with the player, and opens into Spotify, YouTube and Local.
- Search: the magnifier next to each library searches it. Type, press Enter, click a result to play it (Spotify songs, albums and playlists; YouTube videos; your local songs).

## 1.9.21
- A playlist copied from Spotify to your YouTube account now shows once in the playlist panel, not twice.

## 1.9.20
- Copy a playlist between Spotify and YouTube: in the playlist panel, "Copy to YouTube" (under SPOTIFY) or "Copy to Spotify" (under YOUTUBE), then pick the playlist. Each song is looked up by name; the bar under the player shows the progress and can stop it.
- Playing a playlist no longer brings the Spotify window to the front.
- The cassette shows its edge while it turns over.

## 1.9.19
- ALBUM tab next to Lyrics and Artist: the album's songs (the playing one lit up), a short text about the album, and a button that plays the whole album on Spotify.
- Halfway through an album, the cassette turns over to side B and the record slides out of its sleeve, turns round and goes back in.
- About the artist finds the band more reliably when its name is also an ordinary word.

## 1.9.18
- Lyrics and About the artist: the repeat button is now INFO. It opens a panel under the player with the song's lyrics (the sung line lights up when the lyrics are timed) or a short text about the artist.
- Playlists under the album picture: Spotify, YouTube and local playlists in folders that open and close. Click one to play it.
- Shuffle now sticks on Windows: Minapp also sets it through your Spotify connection.
- New Record look, a double-size option and a choice of cover filters (Player > Album picture).
- The cassette's tape winds across as the song plays, and the label flips between side A and B.
- Sleep timer (Player > Sleep timer): pauses after 15 to 90 minutes or after this song. The clock counts down in the last minute.
- Local library > Get missing covers: finds the covers of songs downloaded before 1.9.16.
- New hotkeys: Ctrl+Alt+L likes the song, Ctrl+Alt+I opens lyrics.

## 1.9.17
- Tune-up. With no internet, Minapp no longer asks Spotify for the cover and the heart on every tick; it waits 30 seconds between tries.
- A web request that hangs (sleeping laptop, hotel Wi-Fi) now gives up after 15 seconds instead of freezing the queue or covers.
- Local playlists read each song's cover from disk once, not several times a second.
- The album panel's animations (cassette reels, spinning CD) do about half the work per frame.
- Small clean-ups from a full code check.

## 1.9.16
- Downloads now save each song's cover and album name next to it (MP3s also get the cover built in), so local playlists show the album panel too.
- Local songs without a saved cover say NO COVER straight away.

## 1.9.15
- New album picture filter, like an old GIF: each cover is reduced to its own 32 colours and shaded with fine dots (dithering) instead of blocky pixels.

## 1.9.14
- Cassette box: the album picture now fills the whole card, with the title over a darker band at the bottom.
- The title on the box tilts with the box, letter by letter, so every letter stays clean.

## 1.9.13
- The LOADING animation now also shows while Minapp is still waiting for the album picture's address (Spotify sends it a moment after the song), so the checkers are gone for good.
- If no picture arrives after 10 seconds, the panel says NO COVER instead.

## 1.9.12
- While the album picture loads, the panel shows LOADING with sliding stripes and counting dots instead of checkers.
- The album name is never bigger than the normal letter size; long names use up to three lines.

## 1.9.11
- Album panel restyled. The album name is in a small pixel font: white on the box and the CD, black on the cassette.
- The background takes the player's own colour with a hint of the album's, instead of flat black.
- The cassette box is drawn after a real clear plastic box, turned slightly so its side shows, with the album title under the cover.
- The cassette keeps its real proportions, with the title on a paper strip; the letters get bigger or smaller to fit the name.

## 1.9.10
- New album panel, drawn as pixel art to match the player, with the album's name underneath in the skin's own font. Three looks: a cassette box, a cassette whose reels turn while music plays, and a CD. Click the panel to switch, or use the menu, Album picture.
- Album picture, Spin the CD: the CD turns slowly in pixel steps while music plays (off by default, the cover then stays upright).
- The album name is read from Spotify on both Mac and Windows; YouTube shows the video title.

## 1.9.9
- The album picture now sits in a clear plastic case with the source's logo on top (Spotify or YouTube) and a little sound-wave under the cover that is different for every song.
- Second style: a cassette tape with the cover as its label and reels that turn while music plays. Click the picture to switch between the two; click the song title or timer to show or hide it.

## 1.9.8
- The album picture is sharper: 64 x 64 pixels instead of 16 x 16, with a lightly reduced palette.

## 1.9.7
- No more pop-up notifications from Windows (or the Mac notification center) for updates, likes or finished downloads. The light bulb, the red heart and the download bar show these inside the player. Song-change notifications stay off unless you turn them on under Settings.

## 1.9.6
- Windows: the album picture now comes from Windows' own media controls, where Spotify puts its cover. No login needed.
- Windows: the heart works. Windows does not tell Minapp which Spotify song is playing, so the heart asks Spotify itself (this needs the Spotify connection; the heart offers to set it up).

## 1.9.5
- Catching up after a long gap: the What's new pop-up now lists every version you missed (scroll it with the mouse wheel), including when you come from an old version that did not remember which one you had. The update question and the release page also show the full history, newest first.

## 1.9.4
- Heart button for Spotify: the eject button is now a heart. Click it (or the ADD button in the playlist window) to add the playing song to your Liked Songs; the heart turns red, click again to remove it. The first time, Minapp asks you to connect Spotify once more so it may change your Liked Songs. The menu is still on the O button and right-click.

## 1.9.3
- Spotify cover pictures are now found without any login, also when the Spotify app does not hand over its artwork address.
- The update light bulb now shows up soon after every new release: Minapp checks about every 30 minutes and whenever you bring the player to the front (not just once a day). The checks are tiny and quiet, and you get one notification per version.

## 1.9.2
- Album picture on a Mac now comes straight from the Spotify app, so it no longer needs the queue connection. Clicking the timer only shows or hides the picture and no longer flips the clock between elapsed and remaining time. A missing picture shows a visible grey checker.

## 1.9.1
- Album picture: click the song title or the timer to show a small pixelated picture of the album (Spotify) or the video thumbnail (YouTube) on the left of the player. Click the picture to hide it. Spotify pictures need the Spotify queue to be connected.

## 1.9.0
- Pick a Spotify playlist: press LIST in the playlist window to see your playlists (Liked songs, My playlists, Saved from others) in the skin's own font. Click one to play it.
- If Spotify was connected before, the list asks you to connect once more so Minapp may read your playlists.

## 1.8.9
- Faster and lighter: the visualizer is paced like the original Winamp, which halves its drawing work, and long YouTube playlists no longer resend their whole song list twice a second.
- Go back to an earlier version: Settings, Go back to an earlier version. Minapp keeps the last 3 versions from now on.

## 1.8.8
- The update light bulb now sits over the menu symbol in the top-left corner. Right-click still opens the menu.
- After an update, Minapp shows what's new (this list).

## 1.8.7
- A glowing light bulb appears in the top-left corner when a new version is ready. Click it to update.

## 1.8.6
- Pause and Stop on the download bar now really stop the download.
- New option in the Local library menu: save downloads as MP3 320, which plays in every DJ app. On by default.

## 1.8.5
- Test release for the in-app updater.

## 1.8.4
- Updating from inside the app now works on Windows while Minapp is running.

## 1.8.3
- While saving a YouTube link, a bar under the player shows which song is downloading, with Pause and Stop buttons in the skin's own style.

## 1.8.2
- Mac: the visualizer reacts to the music and Milkdrop runs smoother.
- Widget mode (small and large) and a macOS desktop widget.
- YouTube sign-in to play your own playlists, and an experimental YouTube Music source.

## 1.8.1
- Long YouTube playlists (500+ songs) save completely. When YouTube asks to slow down, Minapp waits and carries on.
- "Check for updates…" in the menu updates Minapp from GitHub.
